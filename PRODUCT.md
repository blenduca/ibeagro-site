# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite (multi-page static HTML + vanilla JS), with Three.js, GSAP/ScrollTrigger and Lenis installed locally via npm. No CDN, no cloud deploy from this workspace. Future hosting: Vercel (Blenduca), not to be done now.

## Users

- Professionals and students who want to enter or grow inside agribusiness chains (dairy, swine, poultry, cooperatives, management, tech) and register interest in the first courses.
- Companies, cooperatives and agroindustries (HR, managers, directors) looking for in-company training, leadership development, corporate education and consultancy.
- Specialists (vets, agronomists, consultants, teachers) who may join the partner-specialist network.
- Latin American audiences reached through the founder's lectures (e.g. Peru) — the site will be shown abroad right after launch.

## Product Purpose

Institutional + commercial site for IBEAGRO — Instituto Brasileiro de Ensino do Agronegócio, headquartered in Chapecó (SC), national reach, Latin America ambition. Three goals: position IBEAGRO as a reference in agro education, capture leads for the first courses (interest lists per course, used to measure demand before the 2027 launch), and generate corporate opportunities.

## Positioning

Born in Chapecó, a city whose economy is the agroindustry and cooperatives of animal protein and dairy. Connects technical knowledge, practical experience and innovation, taught by people who live the sector's challenges. Moves from field to industry, from operation to leadership.

## Operating Context

- Lead capture: interest list per course, corporate proposal, partner specialist, institutional partnership. Forms are front-end only in this version (validation + success state) with a single integration point for a spreadsheet/automation later.
- WhatsApp contact and a free community (WhatsApp group) — numbers not yet defined.
- Course modalities: online, in-person, hybrid, career programs. A course platform (Hotmart today, own platform later) is undecided.
- Future: postgraduate programs (end 2027 / early 2028) — not to be announced as available.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Copy in `COPY DO SITE — IBEAGRO.pdf` is the source; it may be shortened and paraphrased without changing meaning or subject.
- Pages: Início, O IBEAGRO, Formações (+ individual course pages), Para Empresas, Contato. "Experiências" is a home section.
- Course details (hours, specialist, price, next class) are "a confirmar / a divulgar".
- Must not look like or mention VetQuality (IBEAGRO will be a separate company).

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: `Asset 1.svg` (stacked) and `Asset 2.svg` (horizontal) — five panels: bovino (dark green), suíno (red-orange), aves (yellow/gold), equino (blue), plant (green), over an open book with fields, family/cooperative and silos.
- Client direction: seriousness and authority; green, gold and white as base; flat color, no gradients; each species/segment keeps its own color across materials.
- Tagline: "Conhecimento que impulsiona carreiras e transforma o agro."

## Evidence on Hand

Only the copy and the logo. No testimonials, student numbers, partner logos, specialist photos or metrics exist — none may be fabricated. Imagery comes from free-license stock banks.

## Product Principles

1. Seriousness first: authority of an institute, modern but never carnival-colored.
2. Every page converts: each section leads to an interest list, a proposal or a conversation.
3. Teach while presenting: diagrams and interactions explain the agro chains, not decorate.
4. Rooted in Chapecó, open to Latin America.
